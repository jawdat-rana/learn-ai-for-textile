# TEST REPORT (made up, for training only)

**Laboratory:** Example Testing Laboratory Ltd. (fictional)
**Client:** Northcove Apparel (fictional)
**Report no.:** ETL-2025-0417 · **Date:** 12 September 2025

## Sample

- Sample ID: NC-PESBD-01
- Description: polyester staple fibre with biodegradability additive,
  **un-dyed, no finish applied**, supplied by the client
- Reference material: microcrystalline cellulose

## Method

Aerobic biodegradation in seawater, determined by oxygen demand in a closed
respirometer. Test medium: natural seawater, filtered, from a single
location. Temperature **30 ± 1 °C**. Darkness, continuous stirring.
Duration **180 days**. **Three** replicates for the sample, two for the
reference.

## Results

| | Biodegradation after 180 days (% of theoretical) |
|---|---|
| Replicate 1 | 66% |
| Replicate 2 | 64% |
| Replicate 3 | 56% |
| **Sample mean** | **62%** |
| Reference (cellulose) | 84% |

Biodegradation of the sample was still increasing at day 180.

## Conclusion

Under the conditions of this test, the sample showed significant aerobic
biodegradation in seawater.

## Notes and limitations

1. Results apply only to the sample as received and tested.
2. Test conditions do not represent any specific natural environment.
   Results may not be extrapolated to other environments, to dyed or
   finished fabrics, or to blends.
3. Ecotoxicity of degradation residues was not assessed.
4. Disintegration of fabric pieces was not assessed.

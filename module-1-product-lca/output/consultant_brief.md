# Request for quotation: LCA of the P01 Performance T-shirt

*Example output from Prompt 9. Made-up product. Items marked [DECISION] are
for us to decide before sending.*

## 1. Goal and audience

We want to understand the environmental profile of our P01 Performance
T-shirt and test design options. **Intended use:** [DECISION] internal
decisions only, or a public comparative claim. A public comparative claim
needs an ISO 14044 critical review by a panel and will cost more.

## 2. Product and functional unit

- 140 g, 100% polyester filament knit, dyed and sewn in China, sold in the EU.
- Proposed functional unit: one garment worn [DECISION: 50] times over its
  life in the EU, with a second result per wear.

## 3. System boundary

Cradle to grave: fibre, yarn, knitting, dyeing and finishing (including
chemicals and wastewater treatment, which our screening left out), garment
making, packaging, transport, use and end of life. Please state any
cut-off rules and what you exclude.

## 4. Impact categories

Climate change (GWP100, IPCC AR6) as the main indicator, plus water
scarcity, freshwater eutrophication and resource use (fossils). Please
propose a method for microfibre release or say how you'd report it
qualitatively. [DECISION] Do we want a full EU PEF impact set?

## 5. What our screening already found

- About 2.4 kg CO2e per garment (screening, public factors).
- Biggest stage: dyeing and finishing (about 29%), then fibre (22%) and use
  (19%).
- Most sensitive inputs: dye-house heat (MJ per kg), polyester fibre factor,
  yarn extrusion energy.

## 6. Primary data we can supply

Bill of materials, garment weights and cutting waste, transport routes, and
(by [DECISION: date]) energy and fuel data from our dye house and knitting
mill. Please tell us which other data you need from us.

## 7. What we want back

Report to ISO 14044, the model files in an open or agreed format, a data
quality assessment for each key input, a sensitivity analysis on the inputs
above, and results for two design options: [DECISION: recycled polyester;
alternative dye house].

## 8. Questions for every consultant (so quotes are comparable)

1. Which database and version will you use (ecoinvent, Sphera, other)?
2. Is critical review included, and by whom?
3. How will you allocate impacts for recycled polyester?
4. How will you model the use phase and end of life, and from what data?
5. What primary data will you collect yourselves vs expect from us?
6. How will you treat microfibre release and biodegradability?
7. Timeline, number of review rounds, and who owns the model afterwards?
8. Fixed price, and what would cost extra (more products, more options)?

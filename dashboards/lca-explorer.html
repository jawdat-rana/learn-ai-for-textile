Product LCA Explorer

A screening-level carbon footprint (kg CO2e) for six made-up textile products, from fibre to end of life. Pick a product, try what-ifs, and see which numbers matter most.

Screening level, not an ISO 14040/44 study. Factors are rounded public values with wide ranges. Use this to find hotspots and ask better questions, never for claims on a label or website.

Your own file never leaves your browser. It needs the same columns as the sample factors file, so you can swap in your supplier's or database values.

Scorecard 5 = best

Climate and cost scores are relative: the best option gets 5 and the worst 1, even when the gap is small. Hover a score for the reason behind it. Evidence is not in the weighted score. Read it before trusting a high score.

Before you trust this: durability (washes in life) is an assumption, and it moves the per-wear result as much as the fibre choice. Microplastic, water and end-of-life scores are judgement calls written in beyond_carbon.csv. Ask your AI: "Which assumption, if wrong, would change the ranking?"
